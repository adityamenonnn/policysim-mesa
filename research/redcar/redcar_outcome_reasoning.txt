REDCAR 2015 — OUTCOME ESTIMATE REASONING
======================================================================

All estimates are ESTIMATED_LOW data quality.
Mid values represent the most plausible single estimate.
Low/high values represent the plausible range given data uncertainty.
All mid values sum to 100% at each time horizon.


======================================================================
TIME HORIZON: Q1 2016 (5 months post-closure)
AGE BAND: all

HARD ANCHORS USED:
  still_seeking=25% (DWP JSA stats, HIGH). 35% never claimed JSA (unknown split). Remaining 40% left JSA by Feb 2016 (not necessarily employed).

MATH:
  still_seeking FIXED at 25% (DWP). exit MID: 12-18% early retirement (of 35% never-claimed) + ~2-3% early ESA = 20%. retrain MID: 2% — too early for genuine occupational retraining (courses only just starting). share_similar MID: 10% — workers with portable skills (electricians, HGV, maintenance) found work quickly. underemployed MID: 100-25-20-2-10 = 43%. Sum check: 25+20+2+10+43 = 100 ✓

CROSS-EPISODE PRIORS:
  Hartz: re-employment wages fell 10%; 80%+ moved to lower-paying employer → underemployment dominant. Port Talbot 5-month analogue: retraining near zero at 5 months. China Shock: immediate re-employment mainly low-wage (underemployed); high-wage workers → share_similar.

DOMINANT UNCERTAINTY:
  Split of the 35% who never claimed JSA — we cannot tell if they retired, were immediately re-employed, or left the UK.

ESTIMATES:
  retrain:       1-4%  (mid 2%)
  share_similar: 6-15%  (mid 10%)
  underemployed: 30-50%  (mid 43%)
  exit:          12-28%  (mid 20%)
  still_seeking: 25-25%  (mid 25%)
  mid sum: 100
